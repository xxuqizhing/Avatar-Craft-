Privacy Policy

App: Avatar Craft+  |  Effective Date: November 2026

Welcome to Avatar Craft+ ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you use our mobile application.

Summary:
Avatar Craft+ is an offline-capable image editing and avatar creation utility. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identity data, facial biometric data, or original photos. All photo generation, decoration, and processing take place entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in data minimization. The App does not require user registration or login. We do not collect any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or device-specific identifiers).

2. Device Permissions and Usage

To enable core features such as avatar customization and saving, the App may request access to the following device features:

  • Photo Library (Add-Only / Save Permission): Requested when you choose to save an avatar or exported artwork. This permission is solely used to write the resulting image to your Photos app. We do not inspect, upload, or analyze any other photos in your album.
  • Photo Library (Read Permission - Optional): Requested only if you choose to import an existing photo from your library to add stickers or decorations. The selected photo is loaded into local memory for editing and is never sent to any external server.
  • Camera (Optional): Requested only if you take a live picture to edit within the App. Images captured through the camera remain strictly on your local device.

3. Facial Data and Image Processing

For sticker placement, decorative frames, and avatar crafting:

  • All image operations are performed completely on-device via native rendering frameworks.
  • We do not create, extract, or retain biometric templates, landmarks, or face embeddings.
  • Temporary image cache created during the editing workflow is discarded automatically once the session is completed or the App is closed.

4. Third-Party Services and Tracking

The App does not integrate any third-party behavioral analytics, ad-tracking SDKs, or data brokers. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children’s Privacy

Avatar Craft+ is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age limit).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App following any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
